## Why the hashtags are set up this way
- **The first three hashtags show up above your title**, so the description starts with `#Kagurabachi #MangaEdit #Chihiro`. Those are the most searched for this video.
- **Keep the description under 60 hashtags.** Above 60, YouTube ignores every hashtag on the video. This list has 34.
- **Both title hashtags (#Kagurabachi, #Chihiro) are also in the description.**
- **Technique and sword names (#CloudGouger, #Enten, #Kuro, #Nishiki)** catch fans searching the chapter itself, where competition is lower than on #Manga.
- **The Japanese hashtags (カグラバチ, チヒロ, 少年ジャンプ, 漫画)** reach Japanese viewers. The video already uses Japanese type (十二秒, 零距離, カグラバチ).
- The algorithm cares more about watch time and replays than tags. The cut ends on `00:00` and the title card, and the opening is the clock reading `12:00`, so it loops cleanly into a replay.
